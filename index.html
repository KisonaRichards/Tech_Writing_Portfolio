Standard Operating Procedure

Connect a Windows 11 Computer to Wi-Fi

Revision A | Estimated time: 2 minutes
Purpose

To connect a Windows 11 computer to a Wi-Fi network.

Audience

Anyone with a laptop who is not familiar with network settings.

You need:

  • The name of the Wi-Fi network
  • The Wi-Fi password

NOTE: The password is case-sensitive. Capital and lowercase letters must match exactly.

Steps

  1. 1 Click the network icon in the bottom-right corner of the screen, next to the clock.
  2. 2 Click the arrow (>) next to the Wi-Fi button. A list of available networks appears.
  3. 3 Click the name of your network.
  4. 4 Select Connect.
  5. 5 Type the password.
  6. 6 Select Next.
  7. 7 Wait for the word Connected to appear under the network name.

Verification

The procedure is complete when the network icon shows the Wi-Fi symbol and Connected appears under the network name.

If It Does Not Work

Problem What to do
Network name is not in the list Make sure Wi-Fi is turned on and Airplane mode is off.
“Can’t connect to this network” Select the network and choose Forget, then repeat the steps.
Password rejected Re-enter the password and check capital letters.
Still does not work See the troubleshooting flowchart on the next page.

Procedure: Assemble a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

Purpose

To make one peanut butter and jelly sandwich safely and consistently.

Scope

Applies to a home kitchen using a jar of peanut butter, a jar of jelly, and a loaf of bread.

WARNING: Peanut butter can cause severe allergic reactions. Do not prepare or serve this sandwich to anyone with a peanut allergy.

Materials

1 jar peanut butter, 1 jar jelly, 1 loaf bread (2 slices needed)

Equipment

1 plate, 2 butter knives, paper towels, sanitizing wipe

If the bread is not sliced, also get: 1 serrated bread knife, 1 cutting board

Section 1: Prepare the Work Area
  1. Wash and dry your hands.
  2. Wipe the counter with a sanitizing wipe.
  3. Put the peanut butter and jelly on the counter.
  4. Put the bread on the counter.
  5. Put the plate on the counter.
  6. Put the two butter knives on a paper towel next to the plate.
  7. Close all doors and drawers.
Section 2: Prepare the Bread
  1. Open the bread bag.
  2. Check whether the bread is sliced.
    • If sliced: Remove 2 slices and put them flat on the plate. Go to Section 3.
    • If not sliced: Do steps 3 to 5.

WARNING: The bread knife is sharp. Keep your fingers away from the blade.

  1. Put the loaf on the cutting board.
  2. Cut 2 slices, each about 12 mm (½ in) thick, using a back-and-forth sawing motion.
  3. Put the 2 slices flat on the plate, side by side.
  4. Close and secure the bread bag.
Section 3: Add the Fillings

CAUTION: Use a different knife for each filling. This keeps the peanut butter and jelly jars clean.

  1. Remove the lid from the peanut butter jar and set it on the counter.
  2. Scoop about 2 tablespoons of peanut butter with the first knife.
  3. Spread the peanut butter on slice 1, all the way to the edges.
  4. Put the first knife on the paper towel.
  5. Remove the lid from the jelly jar and set it on the counter.
  6. Scoop about 1 tablespoon of jelly with the second knife.
  7. Spread the jelly on slice 2, all the way to the edges.
  8. Put the second knife on the paper towel.
  9. Pick up slice 2 and place it on slice 1 with the jelly side down. Line up the edges.
Section 4: Clean Up
  1. Screw the lids back onto both jars until hand-tight.
  2. Put the jars and bread back in their storage places.
  3. Throw away the paper towel.
  4. Wash the knives and any other tools you used.
Verification

The procedure is complete when:

  • The sandwich has 2 slices with peanut butter and jelly between them, covering the bread edge to edge.
  • Both jars are closed and put away.
  • The work area is clean.
Troubleshooting Logic & Visio Diagram Decision Flowchart

Wi-Fi Connected but No Internet

A flowchart shows decisions visually, which is useful for troubleshooting. Here is the logic. I’ll draw it as a real diagram in the PDF.

Visual Decision Diagram (Visio Layout) Scroll horizontally on small screens ↔

Structured Decision Logic

Start Computer says “Connected” but web pages will not load.
1. Do other devices (phone, tablet) have internet on the same Wi-Fi?
No: The problem is the router or the internet service. Restart the router: unplug it, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in, wait 2 minutes. If it still fails, contact your internet provider. End.
Yes: Go to step 2.
2. Is Airplane mode off?
No: Turn it off. Test again. End.
Yes: Go to step 3.
3. Restart the computer. Does the internet work now?
Yes: End.
No: Go to step 4.
4. Forget the network and reconnect (see Sample 3). Does the internet work now?
Yes: End.
No: Go to step 5.
5. Run the built-in troubleshooter: Settings > System > Troubleshoot > Other troubleshooters > Network and Internet > Run . Follow the on-screen instructions. If the problem remains, contact IT support.
